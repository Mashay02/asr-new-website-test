The paperwork, in plain English

What F-Gas and REFCOM actually mean for you

Nearly all commercial cooling kit holds F-Gas refrigerant. The law says it must be leak-checked at set intervals, by certified engineers, with records kept — and an EHO visit or an insurance claim is exactly when that folder gets asked for. REFCOM is the register that audits cooling companies to prove they handle refrigerant legally.

Ours is simple: every ASR engineer is F-Gas certified, the company is REFCOM registered, and on a maintenance contract we keep your logbook current without being chased. When someone asks for the paperwork, the answer is a folder — not a scramble.
